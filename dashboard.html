What this measures, and what it does not

  • Travel distance only. Pick time, mast raise and lower, scanning, congestion, queueing at the dock and put-away are not in it.
  • Routes start and end at STAGING. Cross-aisles at the front and back only.
  • Level does not change travel distance - the truck stands in the same spot whether the pallet is at floor level or six beams up. Mast time is real and is not measured here.
  • Every figure on this page is computed from the two CSV files at run time. None are hard-coded.
